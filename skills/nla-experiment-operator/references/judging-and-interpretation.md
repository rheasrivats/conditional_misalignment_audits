# NLA judging and interpretation

Read this before creating review packets, running judges, opening reveal keys,
or interpreting NLA results.

## Preserve review order

1. Verify the terminal decoded artifact and source snapshot hashes.
2. Resolve divergent siblings and record their disposition.
3. Freeze a blinded review contract and independent seed.
4. Build anonymous packets and keep reveal keys separately.
5. Freeze observations before reveal.
6. Adapt automated-judge prompts only from permitted development evidence.
7. Freeze prompts, schemas, runtime, seeds, matching keys, retries, egress,
   spending, outputs, missingness, and aggregation.
8. Build fresh independently randomized judge inputs.
9. Preserve, validate, account, and seal before local reveal and analysis.

## Keep judge roles distinct

### Independent description

Measure coherence and topic relation separately from the target construct. Use
axis-level missingness when no stance is attributable. Require source-grounded
evidence for nonzero claims and record protective counter-stances.

### Matched comparison

Compare only within the frozen matching key, such as prompt, context, position,
and decode contract. Permit ties and unscorable outcomes. Keep reliability
side-specific. Do not rank based on fluency, specificity, domain vocabulary, or
presumed condition.

### Bundle or model assessment

Separate NLA fidelity from candidate model-level hypotheses. Require cross-row
support, contradictory/protective evidence, benign explanations, and predicted
elicitation conditions. A decoder-fidelity failure is not a model failure mode.

## Validate provider results locally

- Authorize exact prompt, context, NLA text, judge instructions, schemas, and
  destination before egress.
- Exclude identities, lineage, reveal keys, and behavioral outputs unless
  separately approved.
- Preserve each complete provider body before parsing or validation.
- Treat deterministic HTTP 4xx responses as non-retryable.
- Document every transport-schema projection and enforce the full schema
  locally.
- Validate cross-field semantics, opaque IDs, evidence requirements, and cited
  excerpts as literal source substrings when the scientific claim depends on
  quotation.
- Never repair provider output into acceptance.

## Construct packets content-safely

Bind one authoritative source artifact from terminal manifests and receipts.
Reproduce exact size, row count, and SHA-256. Preserve and exclude divergent
siblings; never substitute or repair them silently.

Treat pair or group matching as scientific configuration. Enumerate candidate
counts and unmatched cells for every plausible key without inspecting
protected text. Approve one key and unmatched policy, then freeze the exact IDs
and coverage receipt. Never fall back to another identifier implicitly.

Audit schemas, fields, hashes, counts, and allowlisted identifiers before any
content-bearing query. If a broad search exposes protected content, stop,
preserve the exposure record, and apply the project's incident workflow.

## Interpret continuation features cautiously

Many last-prompt-token NLA descriptions emphasize answer genre, response
structure, or likely continuation. Treat this as response-plan or continuation
evidence, not automatically as a belief, intention, personality, or stable
policy.

Before broader use, develop fidelity across relevant positions/layers, repeated
descriptions, reconstruction evidence when appropriate, stability, topic
relation, and positive/descriptive anchors. Do not choose a configuration using
protected target comparisons.

## Aggregate hierarchically

Aggregate stochastic descriptions within activation, activations within
prompt, and prompts within condition. Three descriptions of one activation are
not three independent examples. Freeze numeric/null handling, item-level retry
exhaustion, minimum coverage, unmatched cells, and imputation policy before
results.

Report prompt-level heterogeneity, missingness, protective evidence, and
contradictions rather than only pooled row counts. Distinguish the primary
construct from descriptive or secondary axes; do not create an omnibus score
unless that construct is independently defined and validated.

## Bound latent-detection claims

Keep model lineage, conditions, behavior, scores, and reveal keys out of blind
latent-detection payloads. Use incremental baselines—prompt alone, visible
prefix where applicable, NLA plus prompt, matched condition descriptions, and
positive/negative/descriptive anchors—to identify what the judge reads.

With one reference and one adapted checkpoint, describe successful blind
classification as preliminary single-case discrimination. It may reflect an
adapter or stylistic fingerprint. Require independent model replication for a
general detector claim.
